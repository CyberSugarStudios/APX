Step 4: Lock Features

Check anything that's a fixed, defining part of this species -- the player won't be able to remove it (they can still add MORE of the same trait themselves, or adjust anything left unchecked). Things a player might reasonably want to reflavor or opt out of -- like a Flying race whose character has clipped or injured wings, refunding the GP -- are best left unchecked.
